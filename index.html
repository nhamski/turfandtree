About us

No sales pitch, no fine print

Turf & Tree is based in Logan, Kansas, and handles the full range most properties need from three or four different companies — lawn care, irrigation, hydroseeding, and landscaping across Phillips County and every acre within about 30 miles of town, backed by our own tree nursery. We maintain lawns to a standard that holds up past install day. We diagnose and repair irrigation systems — broken heads, cracked lines, controllers that quit talking to the valves — the kind of troubleshooting most crews skip past. We hydroseed bare dirt, road banks, and new builds so they establish thick instead of thin. And we build the outdoor spaces you'll actually use: beds, borders, walkways, shade exactly where you want it. Our nursery grows hardy stock bred to take Kansas wind, heat, and winter, and we ship it nationwide to people who'd rather buy from a grower than a big-box pallet. One accountable crew, a straight answer on what the job takes and what it costs, and work that's finished right the first time.
