About us

No sales pitch, no fine print

Turf & Tree is based in Logan, Kansas — one accountable crew instead of the three or four vendors most properties end up juggling for lawn care, irrigation, hydroseeding, and landscaping, backed by our own tree nursery. We diagnose the irrigation problems most lawn crews skip past — broken heads, cracked lines, controllers that quit talking to the valves — instead of patching the symptom and moving on. Our nursery selects stock proven to handle Kansas wind, heat, and winter, for people who'd rather buy from a grower than a big-box pallet. Serving Phillips County and every acre within about 30 miles of town: a straight answer on what the job takes, what it costs, and work finished right the first time.
